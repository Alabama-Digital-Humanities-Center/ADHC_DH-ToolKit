# Build a Page in Omeka S
#### [Made by Sara Whitver with Scribe](https://scribehow.com/o/Q5Aaj_WQQLSLiZKvgOpJpw/viewer/Build_a_Page_in_Omeka_S__6ArKXOtNRiCViv68CwW_pA)


1\. Start from your administrative dashboard and select the site that you want to add a page to from your "manage sites" quick links. I'm going to add a page to "Sara's Crayfish."

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/4eb38f00-8c70-4df3-9a5e-b1a027d57315/user_cropped_screenshot_b6858ccf18f348fa8c0afc4888e8ff03_text_export.jpeg)


2\. When the "sites" menu appears, select the "pages" link.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/5c423513-11ec-4d68-a6a4-eb7c026ee6f1/user_cropped_screenshot_174c724716584e3f8d084cf4e116b81a_text_export.jpeg)


3\. Click "Add new page"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/01a89137-d3ae-41ea-8859-866aba4f99b7/user_cropped_screenshot_4f56256f9db34ceeb9fc3375c886016c_text_export.jpeg)


4\. Give your page a title and an optional URL slug and make sure to save!

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/ac3faa76-fee3-4587-bebf-bf5bc6dd0366/user_cropped_screenshot_7ccbe6f55d9e4ddf987c1af1aae54e9c_text_export.jpeg)


5\. I like to start with some base organization and select "Add Block Group." This helps keep things together on your page.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/e0796047-25fe-4d8d-92df-4f5539657e7e/user_cropped_screenshot_454d418e6cd64fdb962c83f214bcf940_text_export.jpeg)


6\. Now you can start adding blocks to your page. This is a two-step process, add the block to your page and then drag it by the three line "hamburger" icon into the Block Group.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/1e77a890-ec44-4388-956e-a2dff261d899/user_cropped_screenshot_7803943f5fba4ef4bb4910e9e3276a8c_text_export.jpeg)


7\. In my block group, I am adding an HTML block, an assets block. Then I'm adding a browse preview block below, outside of the block group.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/3a513cb8-6d0d-4192-8aa8-f10bb185efb0/user_cropped_screenshot_db7f1238e877498cbc317b701cf5c251_text_export.jpeg)


8\. You can upload your assets in the process of adding them to your block.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/9c628573-b425-45b0-9937-d88a40b86fc3/user_cropped_screenshot_6139b279f4b944d1a8f3192bdf94bae1_text_export.jpeg)


9\. Type "Zappo is such a cute little creature. He has so many apendages and so many little pinchers and he can use more than one pincher independently from the other. When he's tired, he likes to burrow in the gravel of his cave to hide, and he is always interested in eating peas."


10\. Click "Edit"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/7d5f13a4-53be-4574-9b17-13517582f87c/user_cropped_screenshot_b007d2af3beb46ab84d723b898ac7f68_text_export.jpeg)


11\. In the Browse Preview block, I selected the item set for Zappo the Crayfish.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/1d67f7b5-3644-4978-8182-b311e64550ec/user_cropped_screenshot_b9b6bf4135d1436e9533eeefae09f4c9_text_export.jpeg)


12\. Hooray, you did it! Let's see what it looks like!

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/2c2ffde6-edcc-4ab6-b076-db74610f404e/user_cropped_screenshot_3cb9d5be18de495e8c98abc46e3ecc65_text_export.jpeg)


13\. Click "View"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/dc68cc5e-5fc6-4784-92d7-a57ec57fdbc1/user_cropped_screenshot_76a4c6e8c1c441f794784299b37b79fa_text_export.jpeg)


14\. It's simple but cute. Let's see what you can do with your pages!

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-02/539ab957-8748-42a1-bedf-6b914f84efb9/screenshot_9dab449c75f243839b2ca644de461ddf_text_export.jpeg)
